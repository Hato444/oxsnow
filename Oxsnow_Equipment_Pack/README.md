# Oxsnow equipment assets

Open `index.html` directly in your browser. No installation or internet required.

## Included
- All 10 supplied product photos copied without quality loss into named model folders.
- Each folder has `item.json`. `equipment.json` describes the full requested catalogue.
- Independent sliders, dots, keyboard-accessible buttons, touch swipe, category filters and an enlarged gallery.
- The preview follows the supplied charcoal, black, orange and white design. It is an asset preview, not a modification of your existing site.
- Missing image/model cards have explicit status. No prices, availability, units or hire inclusions have been invented.

## Review and separation
The default preview excludes combined rig photographs and unconfirmed model photographs. Turn on “Show review references” to inspect them. Reference captions identify attachments. These are not clean standalone product photos.

Camera photos include lenses and normal camera handles/viewfinders; lens inclusion is not implied. Microphones, rails and tripod assemblies are flagged as rig references. No object was digitally erased or fabricated. A clean isolated stabiliser image was not provided, and the FX9 tripod rig must not be labelled as a gimbal.

The compact Sony images are held in review: their exact generations are not visible enough to confirm. The side-view FX6-looking photo is also held separately because FX6 is absent from the handwritten list. The FX3 XLR/microphone angle is provisional and excluded by default. Sony FX5 text is retained as supplied.

## OpenCode integration
Copy `assets/` into your project's public/static folder. Adapt each JSON image path to your project's asset base. Use the existing product IDs and booking records when merging: do not replace stock or prices. In normal catalogue mode use only `standalone` and `promotional` images of confirmed models. Port the gallery behaviour from `gallery.js` into the site's current framework. Keep review-only models out of live inventory until confirmed.

`missing-equipment.csv` lists remaining product photos and manufacturer references. References do not establish permission to republish images. RED and stabiliser models, Sound Devices/Tascam handwriting and the wireless-kit regional suffix still need checking.

The package does not contain your original website source, so its booking system has not been edited. Do not deploy this preview as a replacement for the whole Oxsnow site.
